# Glossary

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

**Signal** — a lightweight expression of interest (silent expiry, no decline). **Connection** — the post-signal
protocol instance. **Selfie exchange** — sequential ephemeral photo validation. **Mutual validation** — both approve.
**Connection Ticket** — a held opportunity (2h/24h). **Mission Meet** — short single-objective chat to pick a place.
**Mission Mode** — invisible, one-connection focus. **Cooldown** — non-punitive pause after a mission. **Mood dot** —
super-ready/open/exploring. **Destiny Connection** — coarse repeated-crossing prompt (post-V1). **Pulse** — aggregated
activity zones. **Entitlement** — a typed right derived from purchases/subscriptions. **ActiveUserLock** — DB lock
guaranteeing one active connection per user. **pairKey** — order-independent pair identity. **purgeAt** — hard-delete
due date.
