# Content & Microcopy

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Voice: calm, plain, reassuring; active voice; sentence case; a control names exactly what it does and keeps that name
through the flow. Never announce rejection.

Samples (EN): Signal — "Send a Signal" / "Someone nearby wants to discover you." Selfie — "Send a live selfie" /
"Let them know it's really you." / "Visible only for this Wingman." Secondary "Let it expire" (never "Ignore"/"X").
Mission Meet — "Decide where to meet" / blocked contact: "Contact details aren't allowed here." Cooldown — "Take your
time." Confirmed — "Connection confirmed."

FR mirrors these; both are in the prototype's i18n table. Errors explain what happened and how to proceed, in the
interface's voice, never apologetic or vague.
