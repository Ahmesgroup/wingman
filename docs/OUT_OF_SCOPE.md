# Out of Scope

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Not built, by design: public photo galleries, profile browsing/search, unlimited matching, persistent DM inbox,
feeds, stories/content, gamified streaks, read receipts that pressure engagement, precise-location sharing, and any
"who's online" catalogue. Anything whose main effect is more time-in-app without more real meetings is rejected.
