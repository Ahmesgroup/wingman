# Competitive Positioning

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

**Wingman is a real-world connection facilitator. It helps two people who are already near each other safely discover
mutual interest and say hello in real life.** From presence to hello.

Tinder/Bumble optimize matches → more matches; nobody has to meet. Wingman optimizes a single real approach then
returns you to the Radar. Wingman does not help you collect matches. Wingman helps you make the first acquaintance.
Positioning axes: **discreet** (no public profiles/photos), **private** (approximate
location, per-purpose consent), **real-world** (short, single-objective meet), **non-addictive** (no feed, capped
signals, cooldown). The brand reads as a trustworthy, premium facilitation tool, not a hookup catalogue.
