# Competitive Positioning

**Status:** Owner locked (V3.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

**Category: Social Interaction Facilitation Technology.**  
**Primary tagline: Make the first acquaintance easy.**

Wingman facilitates the first real-world interaction between people who are already near each other — or who repeatedly
cross paths through Destiny Connection. “Real-world connection facilitator” is explanatory wording only; it is not the
category. *Love is in the air.* is the secondary emotional slogan.

Tinder/Bumble optimize matches → more matches; nobody has to meet. Wingman optimizes a single real approach then
returns you to the Radar. Wingman does not help you collect matches. Wingman helps you make the first acquaintance.
Positioning axes: **discreet** (no public profiles/photos), **private** (approximate
location, per-purpose consent), **real-world** (short, single-objective meet), **non-addictive** (no feed, capped
signals, cooldown). **One Connection at a Time.** is a product operating principle, not the main brand promise. The
brand reads as a trustworthy, premium facilitation tool, not a hookup catalogue.
