# Personas

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Fictional personas to guide design and testing (no real data).

**Léa, 27 — "the hesitant regular."** Goes out weekly, notices people, rarely approaches. Wants a low-risk way to
signal interest. Values discretion and no public profile. Success = one real coffee, not 40 matches.

**Marc, 31 — "the intentional."** Busy, dislikes swipe fatigue. Turns Wingman on only when genuinely available.
Uses "Available now" and Super-ready mood. Wants the meeting decided fast.

**Sam, 24 — "the explorer."** Curious, not urgent. Uses "Just exploring" and white mood. Browses the Radar for the
feeling that someone's near; converts occasionally.

**Nadia, 29 — "the safety-first."** Cares about privacy and control. Reads consent screens, keeps Destiny off,
values instant silent blocking and no precise location.
