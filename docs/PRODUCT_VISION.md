# Product Vision

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

**From presence to hello.**

Wingman is a real-world connection facilitator. It helps two people who are already near each other safely discover
mutual interest and say hello in real life—without public exposure, swiping, explicit rejection, or prolonged
digital conversation. It lowers the emotional cost of the first "hello" and then gets out of the way.

The measure of success is real meetings, not engagement time. Wingman deliberately declines the mechanics that make
dating apps sticky (infinite feeds, unlimited matches, endless chat) because those maximize time-in-app instead of
meetings. Wingman does not help you collect matches. Wingman helps you make the first acquaintance.

Long term, Wingman aims to be the trusted, discreet layer for the first acquaintance — recognizable by its calm,
private, magnetic character rather than by loud "who's available" catalogues.
