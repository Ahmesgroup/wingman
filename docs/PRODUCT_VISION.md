# Product Vision

**Status:** Owner locked (V3.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

**Category:** Social Interaction Facilitation Technology  
**Primary tagline:** *Make the first acquaintance easy.*  
**Secondary emotional slogan:** *Love is in the air.*

Wingman facilitates the first real-world interaction between people who are already near each other — or who repeatedly
cross paths through Destiny Connection — without public exposure, swiping, explicit rejection, or prolonged digital
conversation. “Real-world connection facilitator” is explanatory wording only, not the product category. It lowers
the emotional cost of the first acquaintance and then gets out of the way.

The measure of success is real meetings, not engagement time. Wingman deliberately declines the mechanics that make
dating apps sticky (infinite feeds, unlimited matches, endless chat) because those maximize time-in-app instead of
meetings. **One Connection at a Time.** is an operating principle, not the public brand promise.

Long term, Wingman aims to be the trusted, discreet layer for the first acquaintance — recognizable by its calm,
private, magnetic character rather than by loud "who's available" catalogues.
