# Executive Summary (EN)

**Product:** Wingman · **Version:** V3.1 · **Status:** owner locked · **Owner:** Igor Chernikov  
**Category:** Social Interaction Facilitation Technology  
**Primary tagline:** *Make the first acquaintance easy.*  
**Secondary emotional slogan:** *Love is in the air.*

## Product in one sentence

Wingman facilitates the first real-world interaction between people who are already near each other — or who
repeatedly cross paths through Destiny Connection — without swiping, public profiles, endless chat, or explicit
rejection. “Real-world connection facilitator” is explanatory wording only, not the category.

## What Wingman is not

It is not a social network, profile catalogue, infinite messenger, content platform, or swipe app. Any feature that
increases time in the app without increasing the chance of a real interaction is rejected. **One Connection at a Time.**
is an operating principle, not the main brand promise.

## Locked V3.1 product decisions

- Current intention is **Available now / Just exploring**.
- Client Consent for Data Exchange follows identity verification; no feature unlocks first.
- Radar moods are Super ready (red), Open (amber), and Exploring (white), with shape and animation support.
- A captured selfie carries the exact capture timestamp.
- Free allows one active Ticket for up to 2h; Wingman+ allows two active Tickets for up to 24h and one renewal.
- Wingman+ allows **25 Signals/day**.
- Destiny Connection is private, separate opt-in, and off by default.
- Core Principles contain no rigid KPI formula; confirmed real-world meetings remain the north-star outcome.
