# Go-To-Market

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Launch in one dense EU city where nightlife/campus density creates real proximity. Seed with events and venues where
Pulse "Electric" zones are plausible. Message the differentiator: *no swiping, no public profiles, no rejection — just
an easy first hello.* Growth is gated by real-meeting quality, not vanity engagement. Expand city-by-city as Pulse
reaches critical mass.
