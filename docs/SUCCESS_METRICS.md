# Success Metrics

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

## North star
**Confirmed real-world meetings**, tracked as an outcome and read alongside the rates below (not a single rigid
formula — DECISION_LOG D-024).

## Secondary
| Metric | Formula | V1 target |
|---|---|---|
| Daily active users | active/day | >100 at M+3 |
| Signal acceptance | accepted ÷ received | >30% |
| Selfie response | returned ÷ received | >60% |
| Mutual approval | approvals ÷ exchanged | >40% |
| Mission Mode rate | entered ÷ connections | >95% |
| Meeting confirmation | "Let's meet" ÷ Mission Meet opened | >40% |
| Meet outcome (indicative) | meetings ÷ signals sent | >5% |
| Report rate | reports ÷ active sessions | <0.5% |
| D7 retention | active D+7 ÷ registered D0 | >25% |
| Destiny conversion (post-V1) | accepted ÷ prompts | tracking |

Analytics must never contain sensitive user text (AI_CODING_RULES #18).
