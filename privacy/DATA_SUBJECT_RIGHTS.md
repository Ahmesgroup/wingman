# Data Subject Rights

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Access (export on request), rectification (edit profile anytime), erasure (account deletion within 72h, subject to
narrow legal retention), portability (JSON export), and consent withdrawal (per purpose, via Settings). Requests are
tracked in `PrivacyRequest`. A DPO is designated before public launch.
