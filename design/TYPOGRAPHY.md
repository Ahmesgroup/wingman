# Typography

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Display **Sora** (sparingly), interface **Manrope**, mono **JetBrains Mono** (admin/logs/timestamps). Scale:
displayLarge 36/700 (−0.5), heading 24/700, body 16/400, label 14/600 (+0.5). Sora is reserved for big titles and
emotional moments; Manrope carries the interface for calm legibility on small text.
