# Design Principles

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

1. Quiet before connection; emotional only at mutual validation.
2. Information never by color alone (mood dots use shape + motion).
3. Abstract but legible Radar — sense of proximity without exact position.
4. Diffuse Signal, never a targeting line.
5. Calm timers (text + thin bar), no anxiety stack.
6. Motion is normalized via tokens; respect reduce-motion.
7. Rose is rare and reserved for Connection Confirmed.
