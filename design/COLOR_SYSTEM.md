# Color System

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Authoritative values in `design-tokens.json`. Night `#0B1020`, radar `#080D1A`; wingman violet `#7C5CFC`, air
`#B9A7FF`, love `#FF7DAE`; moods red `#FF4D67` / amber `#FFC857` / white `#F4F5F7`. Status colors (feedback) are kept
distinct from mood colors and are always paired with shape/animation so meaning is never carried by hue alone. Rose
is reserved for Connection Confirmed.
