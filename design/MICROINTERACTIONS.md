# Microinteractions

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Radar: slow 3s self-pulse; super-ready dots get a second concentric ring. Signal: diffuse omnidirectional wave from
the button; targeted dot resonates (glow widens, pulses twice) — no straight line. Timer: bar shifts white → amber
→ red with a single 30s haptic derived from server time. Confirmed: violet halos slide and fuse into rose, title
rises, doubleSoft haptic. All disabled/simplified under reduce-motion.
