# Design Vision

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Night, premium, calm, discreet, magnetic — the visual opposite of hot-gradient dating apps. It should feel like the
privacy of a modern banking app, the nocturnal mood of a music app, and the softness of a wellbeing product, with the
precision of a real-time tool. Emotion is withheld until mutual interest is confirmed.
