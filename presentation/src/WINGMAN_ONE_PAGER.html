Wingman

Wingman is a real-world connection facilitator. It helps two people who are already near each other safely discover mutual interest and say hello in real life.

Not a dating app

No public profile browsing · no swipe feed · no endless chat. Free: 2 Signals/day · 1 active connection · ~15 min Mission Meet.

Loop

Radar → Signal → Selfie A/B → Mutual validation → Mission Meet → Cooldown → Radar

Status (Aug 2026)

A Backend V1 GO · protocol frozen   B Intelligence engines flagged · payments OFF · Destiny not public   C Live Field Test S27–S34 in progress

Why it matters

People notice each other every day and say nothing. Existing apps solve remote discovery; Wingman solves the first real-world approach — privately and intentionally.

Safety & privacy

Mutual consent · silent expiry · approximate location · ephemeral selfies · block/report · anti-contact filter. Does not guarantee IRL safety.
