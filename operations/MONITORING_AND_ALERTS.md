# Monitoring & Alerts

**Status:** Decided (V4.1) · Part of the Wingman product & engineering spec.

Dashboards for the signal→meeting funnel, timer expiries, purge health, media-deletion success, moderation queue, and
auth anomalies. Alerts on purge lag, media-deletion failures, moderation spikes, error-rate and latency SLOs, and
Redis memory. No sensitive payloads in telemetry.
